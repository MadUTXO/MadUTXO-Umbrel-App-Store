Before you rotate

  • Queue first, then restart Onionator from Umbrel. Rotation runs during startup; do not restart the target app.
  • Only the selected app's Tor sidecar restarts. The app stays running.
  • The old address may stay reachable until Tor publishes the new descriptor. Share the new address only after it works.
  • Links, bookmarks, paired clients and integrations that use the old address will stop working. Some apps may require a new login.
  • This tool does not rotate Umbrel's dashboard or authentication onions, and does not show app onion addresses.
  • Keep the Umbrel login enabled. This page uses the standard Umbrel app proxy login.
